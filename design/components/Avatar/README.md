A member's face: a colored disc with simple marks in `ink`. Every member gets one, and it appears wherever their name does.

Sizes: `.bq-avatar` 48px (rows), `--sm` 32px (inline, "anotado por"), `--lg` 72px (sheets), `--xl` 144px (winner screen). `--selected` adds the ink ring used when picking a member. Add `.bq-avatar__crown` inside the avatar for the week's leader or winner only.

You provide the SVG from `assets/Avatars/` (inline or via `<img>`) and the member's name as its accessible label. Do not put initials, photos or status dots on an avatar.
